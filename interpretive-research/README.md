## Background
The name WGE comes from the German term _Wirkungsgeschichte_, coined by philosopher Hans-Georg Gadamer in his book _Vreiheit und Methode / Truth and Method_. Here, I abbreviate _Wirkungsgeschichte_ as _WG_. 
Gadamer's work has been crucial to establishing a host of follow-on postmodern interpretive theories in numerous fields of inquiry, including history, literature, and philosophy.

The Interpreter's Horizon: There is ultimately no way for an interpreter to directly interpret a text, material object, or historical event in isolation, as it is; there is no pure, direct access to real objects and events. Each new interpretation, translation, or re-description is performed on the basis of/in a matrix of prior interpretations, individual and community conditioning, life context, and any other historical or present influence that impinges on the "interpretive event."
2. The Text Horizon: The text is a product of all prior influences, and is prospective to all that it will influence. There is ultimately no way for a text to come to us without mediating influences that may or may not already be influences by the text.
